Defrag Conference, the backchannel

2010-11-18

I have spent the last day and a half(ish) at Defrag 2010 in Broomfield, CO. From many prior attendees, I have heard that it is the sort of event that can break your brain (in the best way possible).

There have been a few sessions that I would consider brain-breaking, and most I would consider compelling. One of the joys of the event is monitoring the "back channel" of communication that is happening via twitter.

While I will post some thoughts on specific elements of the event in the next several days, for now I encourage you to glance at the #defragcon tag on twitter or, after the jump, I have embedded a javascript widget that will grab my personal tweets about Defrag and the Technology4Good Barcamp that occurred last evening.
