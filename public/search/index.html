How it works

  1. Ahead of time, ClickHouse's aiEmbed turned every page, talk, demo and photo caption into 384 numbers, using EmbeddingGemma running on my laptop.
  2. When you search, a Cloudflare Worker runs the same model on your words.
  3. Your browser ranks the pages by how close their numbers are to yours. Press ▶ run and the same query runs on play.clickhouse.com instead.
