The times, they are a changin'

2009-06-19

Notice the use of the ' instead of a g...I feel so provincial

Consider this just an advisory notice that my blog is in process of being moved. The transition should be pain free for most as FeedBurner should migrate to the new feed without difficulty. For those of you who read the blog via the web-site. You will be redirected from my blogspot address to this domain shortly.

Why you ask? Quite frankly, control. If there are problems with the site being accessible, comments working, display woes, I want to be able to control them. With blogger I had no control.

Same content, same purpose, new domain. You will see several tweaks over the upcoming days (template, layout, etc) All problems should be momentary...but feel free to comment if there is anything I can do to make the experience more pleasant.
