Project Completion -or- Where has Tyler been?

2011-03-28

You may notice that I have been quiet as of late...

There is, in fact, a good reason.

On March 8, 2011 at 1242 hours Jocelyn (my wife) completed a 9 month sprint with the delivery of our second daughter.

Aoife Liron Hannan was 9 pounds, 9 ounces and now (at nearly 3 weeks old) is a bundle of chubby cheeked love.

As you can imagine, the project was completed on-time but over budget...I suspect that ongoing maintenance will be wildly over budget...and I couldn't be more delighted.

Now that things have settled down, somewhat, I'm back onto a somewhat more consistent schedule of ruminating on technology, payments, and the intersection of the two...which we call "commerce".

Forgive a proud father for including a few more photos.
