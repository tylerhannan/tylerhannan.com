BAI Retail Delivery Conference

2007-11-15

Several of us from IP Commerce are attending the BAI Retail Delivery conference in Vegas this week. . .

Over the next few days, and in somewhat more digestible chunks, I will blog about technology that I have found impressive from the show floor.  For example, Microsoft is showing an impressive Silverlight application for online banking capabilities.

If you are at the show, feel free to swing by booth 1068 to say "Hi". . .I've been having some interesting conversations regarding the resurgence of bank technology solutions and the impact of social network and social personal finance on the marketplace.
