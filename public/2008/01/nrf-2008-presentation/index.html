NRF 2008: Presentation

2008-01-17

As I mentioned earlier in the week, I had the opportunity to present in the Microsoft booth at this year's NRF.  Over the next few days, I will be blogging about some of the more interesting booths and presentations at the show.

In the interim, I have embedded the slides from this presentation after the jump.  As stated on January 11th, the presentation abstract was:

Microsoft has partnered with IP Commerce to securely deliver diverse payment services to retailers. Learn how to expand payment options while reducing integration costs. Hear why the terms PCI, PA-DSS and QSA should not cause uneasiness within retail organizations, and how security compliance can be expedited through software solutions.

It is sometimes difficult to divine all of the content of a presentation from a set of slides (at least when using the presentation style adopted for this show).  As such, please feel free to post a comment or reach out to me directly if you have any specific questions. . .
