An Odd Experience: aka “Why is there a bus in Starbucks?”

2008-06-06

I have spent much of the last week focusing on preparing content for the upcoming training events…My office mate, in an effort to keep me from grumbling too much, recommended a quick Starbucks.

Caffeine is my friend.  My close friend.

Imagine our surprise when we reached the main level of the building (our office is just above a Starbucks) to find a bus parked in the front door.  Yes, you heard right, a bus was parked in the Starbucks.  It would appear that there was a parking brake failure.  I just prefer to think that it was warm enough out that the bus was in search of a venti iced latte.
